# Add Your Key

**Ava runs on your own AI provider key** — free and open source, no account, no card. You pay your provider directly, at their price, and the key stays on your machine.

**Start with Qwen.** One key covers chat, images, voice and video. Make it in the **Singapore** region (the switch at the top of Model Studio) — Ava uses the international endpoint, and a key from another region is refused.

| Provider | Get a key |
|---|---|
| **Qwen (Alibaba Model Studio)** | [Singapore key page](https://modelstudio.console.alibabacloud.com/ap-southeast-1/settings/api-key) |
| **DeepSeek** | [platform.deepseek.com](https://platform.deepseek.com/api_keys) |

*Optional:* a [Brave Search](https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/app/keys) key gives Ava better web search — without one she uses DuckDuckGo. Prefer your own hardware? Point her at Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM instead.

## Add your key

1. Open the Ava dashboard — onboarding asks for it on first run
2. Paste the key and press **Test & save** — Ava checks it before saving it
3. Or later: **Settings → Keys** in the dashboard
