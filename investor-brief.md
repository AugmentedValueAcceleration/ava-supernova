## What Makes Ava Different

### 1. Memory That Actually Works

Five-layer memory system:
1. **Extract** — pulls key facts from conversations in real-time
2. **Reflect** — LLM analyses what was learned after each session
3. **Accumulate** — cross-session pattern detection (you always do X after Y)
4. **Analyse** — connects insights across memories (your preference for X relates to your past with Y)
5. **Consolidate** — merges, deduplicates, and strengthens over time

This isn't "save a note." This is an AI building a genuine understanding of who you are.

### 2. States of Thought — Not Just Modes

Six modes, each a different *way of thinking*:

- **Work** — Builder. Full 54-tool access, code-first.
- **Plan** — Architect. Read-only, evidence-based, strategic.
- **Chat** — Friend. No tools, just conversation. Remembers your life.
- **Teach** — Tutor. Socratic method, curriculum generation, free for everyone.
- **Security** — Auditor. OWASP scanning, CVE research, verification before reporting.
- **Brainstorm** — Ideator. Research, challenge, refine.

Ava reads the room and switches automatically. No manual toggle needed.

### 3. Personas — 24 Specialists, One Agent

Each mode has an internal team. Work mode: Scout, Architect, Verifier, Sequencer, Challenger, Builder. Teach mode: Curriculum Architect, Content Writer, Fact Checker, Quiz Master, Tutor.

One agent, 24 hats. The Conductor orchestrates them based on the task. Users never see the complexity — they just see better results.

### 4. Local-First, Open-Source

All data saves locally by default. Memory, journal, tasks, settings — on your machine. Cloud sync is opt-in, delta-tracked, user-controlled.

The entire agent engine is open source. Users can read the code, modify it, host it themselves. Trust through transparency.

### 5. Education Without a Price Tag

Teach mode is free. No account required. No token limit on learning.

Ava generates personalised curriculums, teaches with the Socratic method, creates quizzes with spaced repetition, and adapts to your level. Every subject. Every language. Every person.

This is the most important feature we'll ever build.

## The Endgame

Ava is not a coding tool.

The coding agent is the entry point — the hardest, most technically demanding use case. If you can build an AI that writes code, reads code, manages files, runs terminals, searches the web, generates images, creates documents, tracks tasks, maintains a journal, teaches you new skills, and remembers everything about you — you can build anything.

**The endgame is JARVIS.**

A personal AI that:
- Manages your calendar and tasks
- Tracks your health and wellness
- Handles your email and communications
- Controls your smart home
- Manages your finances
- Remembers every conversation you've ever had with it
- Gets better every single day

We're building the operating system for human-AI partnership. The coding agent is proof of concept. The companion app is the bridge to daily life. The IDE is the flagship. The business platform (internal, not public) runs the company itself on Ava.

Every surface we add makes the others more valuable. Every memory Ava saves makes her more useful tomorrow. Every user who stays makes the next user's experience better through shared learning (anonymised, opt-in, 3-confirmation threshold before global adoption).
